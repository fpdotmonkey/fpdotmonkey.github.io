I was asked by the Los Angeles FIRST Tech Challenge organization to give a workshop on engineering design to high school students at the annual kickoff for the new season of competitive high school robotics. I gave two sessions of a 45 minute presentation on how to create innovative components for their robots to an audience of a few dozen students in each session.

{{< figure src="/ox-hugo/workshop_in_progress.png" caption="<span class=\"figure-number\">Figure 1: </span>Students look on to my presentation as they learn about engineering design" >}}

[These](/ox-hugo/making_innovative_designs.pdf) are the slides for the presentation I gave.  The topic was how to design innovative systems, especially for the robots that the students will be building for their competition.  I went over how to create a design specification given a design problem (an essential step for designing any system, innovative or not), defining what and innovation even is, identifying when it's useful or not to innovate, and throughout I showed off useful tools to try to understand design.
