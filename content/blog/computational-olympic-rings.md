I've heard that the Olympic Rings logo's colors are based on the colors of the world's flags. With this project, I sought to find out if you would get a similar logo if you captured the colors of the world's flags and made the Olympic Rings logo with them.  I wrote [this article](/ox-hugo/olympic_rings.pdf) to determine if that's true.  Turns out, it is!
