Vine Robots

16 March 2024 mechanical pneumatic soft-robot uc-santa-barbara
In 2019, I worked with the Hawkes Lab at UC Santa Barbara on developing a tool mount for their soft vine-like robots. I ultimately made two prototypes. The first, seen below, is a set of three inflated plastic tubes bound together with magnets that can extend and retract. A tool attached to a plate on the end which mated to the pnuematic structure with a piece of sugical tubing that was gripped by pressure from the tubes. Read more...
